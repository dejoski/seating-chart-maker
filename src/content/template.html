Prefer a spreadsheet? It opens in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers. Fill in Name, Group, Meal and Notes, then use Import CSV in the tool.

These are minimums (guests ÷ seats, rounded up). If you have a head or sweetheart table, subtract those guests first. Many planners add one spare table for late RSVPs and vendors.

Table sizes at a glance

  • 60-inch round: usually 8 comfortably, 10 snug.
  • 72-inch round: usually 10, up to 12 snug.
  • 6-foot banquet: 6 along the sides, 8 with one at each end.
  • 8-foot banquet: 8 along the sides, 10 with ends.

Venues and rental companies vary, so confirm the counts for your chairs and place settings.

Alphabetical or by table?

An alphabetical template (name → table) is quickest for guests to scan, especially above about 80 people. A by-table template (table → names) is what your planner and caterer want. The PDF export includes both, plus a floor plan.

Template questions

Is there an Excel seating chart template?

Yes, the blank CSV above opens in Excel. Once your names are in the tool you can also export a filled-in CSV with table, name, meal and notes.

Can I change a template after loading it?

Yes. Rename tables, change seat counts (1–20), delete tables or add new ones under Tables, and drag tables around the floor plan.

Will loading a template delete my guest list?

No. It replaces the tables and clears seat assignments, but keeps every name and rule. Use Undo if you change your mind.

Are the templates free to print?

Yes. The PDF and PNG exports are free, with a small “made with” footer.
