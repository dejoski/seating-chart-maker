Your data stays in your browser

  • Guest and student names, groups, meals, notes, rules and table layouts are saved only in your browser’s local storage on this device.
  • Nothing you type into the tool is sent to a server. PDFs, PNGs and CSV files are generated on your device.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics scripts and no tracking cookies.
  • Clicking “Get it free” or “Buy” on the Print Kit or Teacher Pack sends an anonymous count (which button, which add-on, which page) to osceolasodsolutions.top so the add-ons’ demand can be measured. No names or chart data are sent.
  • The site is static and served by a hosting provider (currently GitHub Pages, later Cloudflare Pages), which, like any web host, may keep standard server logs such as IP address and pages requested.

Deleting your data

Click “New chart” in the tool, or clear this site’s data in your browser settings. Backup files you download are yours to keep or delete.

Paid add-ons

The Print Kit and Teacher Pack are free for now. Payments are not open yet, so clicking “Buy” never charges anything; it unlocks your free copy. If you type an email into a “notify me” box, that address is stored only to tell you when payments open.

If paid add-ons are offered later, payment is handled by Stripe on Stripe’s own checkout page; this site never sees your card details. Unlocking an add-on stores a flag in your browser’s local storage.

Privacy questions

Can anyone else see my seating chart?

No. It exists only on your device unless you share an exported file yourself.

Will my chart be there next time?

Yes, on the same browser and device, unless you clear site data or use private browsing. Download a backup to be safe.
